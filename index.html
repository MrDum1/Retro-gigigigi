About Retro Gigigi

Welcome to Retro Gigigi! This is a simple web application that allows you to take a picture or upload an image, and then split it into puzzle pieces for you to solve. You can adjust the number of puzzle pieces by changing the columns and rows.

To get started, either take a picture using your camera or upload an image from your device. Then, click the "Make My Puzzle You Peasant!" button to generate the puzzle pieces. Enjoy!
